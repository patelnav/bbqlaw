Your probe's temperature.
On your phone. Off the cloud.

A free iOS app for wireless BLE meat thermometers. Your iPhone reads the probe directly over Bluetooth — no account, no sign-in, no servers in the middle. Just the number you're cooking to.

How it works

Connect
Wake your probe and BBQlaw finds it over Bluetooth LE.
Watch
Live temperature and battery, big and readable, in °F or °C.
Get pinged
Set a target and your phone alerts you when the food hits it — even when the app is closed.
Built for cooks who don't want another login. Questions: hello@bbqlaw.app
