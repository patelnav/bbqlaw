How it works

Connect

Wake your probe and BBQlaw finds it over Bluetooth LE. No vendor app, no login.

Watch

Live temperature and battery — big, readable, in °F or °C — from across the yard.

Get pinged

Set a target and your phone alerts you when the food hits it — even when the app is closed.

Optional · OpenClaw bridge

Hand the cook to your agent.

Forward live readings so your own OpenClaw agent can watch the cook and message someone when it's done. Your phone stays the Bluetooth bridge — the reading never goes through anyone else's account.
