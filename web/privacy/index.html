Privacy Policy

Effective June 2026

BBQlaw is built to keep your cooking on your device. No account, no tracking, no ads. There is no sign-in, no analytics or advertising SDKs, and BBQlaw does not track you across apps or websites.

Local by default

Your iPhone reads the thermometer directly over Bluetooth. Temperatures, targets, probe names, and cook settings stay on your device. To arm the alarm, BBQlaw also writes your target back to the thermometer itself — nowhere else.

The OpenClaw bridge (optional, opt-in)

If — and only if — you tap “Link to your OpenClaw,” BBQlaw connects to our relay at bbqlaw.app and forwards your live cook so your own AI agent can read it. What's sent:

  • Current temperature, target, “to go,” and connection status
  • Probe name and color, and probe/base battery levels
  • A randomly generated device identifier and a reader token

This is not tied to your name, email, or any account — there are none. The relay keeps only the latest reading and a short rolling history, keyed to that random token. Anyone you give the share link to can read that cook. Tap Unlink anytime to revoke the token and stop forwarding.

What we never ask for

BBQlaw never requests your name, email, contacts, location, or photos. Bluetooth access is used solely to talk to your thermometer.

Children

BBQlaw is rated 4+ and collects no personal data.
